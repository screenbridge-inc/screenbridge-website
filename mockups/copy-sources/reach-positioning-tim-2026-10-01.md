# Reach positioning, in Tim's words (voice memo, 2026-10-01)

Source for the "Why run CTV through Reach" copy in `../2026-10-01-v2-ads-plus-reach/`.
Competitor names from the memo are deliberately left off the page.

1. **The creative is included in the media buy.** No creative risk upfront. CTV-quality creative
   built to perform. No shoot, no agency, no new in-house team. Refined and honed across hundreds
   of examples, verticals, and brands; we know what is working right now. This is all we do.
2. **The flywheel.** We run the campaign, get the data every day, week, and month, and iterate the
   creative: variations, angles, messaging, new concepts. With separate agencies the data is not
   transparent and the creative team has to interpret it. We run the campaign, interpret the data,
   and apply it to new creative. One team instead of a media agency, a creative agency, and an
   in-house team.
3. **Buying directly from supply.** Media bought directly through Magnite. A Magnite fee, our fee on
   top, and that is it. Transparent. CPMs competitive or better.
4. **Deals.** Specific channels, programs, networks, or targeting through Magnite and brokers.
   Premium or budget inventory as the strategy calls for; premium content tends to bring premium
   results. A custom media strategy built around the goals and the creative, not a campaign
   switched on.
5. **Attribution.** Innovid pixel. Most of the same attribution metrics the big self-serve
   platforms report. Connectors are still being built out.
