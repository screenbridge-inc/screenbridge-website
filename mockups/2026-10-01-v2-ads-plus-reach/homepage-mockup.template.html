Why run CTV through Reach

One team makes the ad, buys the TV, and learns from both.

A media agency buys your CTV over here. A creative agency builds the ads over there. Your in-house team sits in between, trying to read a report that was never built for them. Reach removes the handoffs.

The creative is in the media buy.

No creative risk upfront. You get CTV-quality ads built to perform, honed across hundreds of spots, verticals, and brands, so we know what is working right now. You do not shoot, hire, or guess.

The campaign teaches the creative.

We run the campaign and read the results every day, week, and month. Then we turn them into variations, new angles, new messaging, and new concepts. Nothing is lost between the people buying the media and the people making the ad, because they are the same people.

Bought directly from supply.

Media is bought directly through Magnite. One exchange fee, one Screenbridge fee, and that is the whole price. Competitive CPMs you can actually see, with nothing hidden in the rate.

The inventory you want, not the inventory you are given.

Specific networks, programs, or premium placements through direct deals. Premium or budget, as the strategy calls for, because premium content tends to bring premium results. A media plan built around your goals and your creative, not a campaign switched on.

Attribution you can act on.

An Innovid pixel ties impressions to site visits and conversions, so the flight report says what the money did and what to change next.
