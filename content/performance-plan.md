# We make the ads. You pay as they run.

No upfront production fee. We build your CTV ads, you run them, and the plan is billed as a share of the media spend they run on.

## How it works {.loop}

Three steps, then it repeats.

### Build

We make a batch of CTV ads from your social content.

### Run

You run them on your streaming buy, tagged so we both see the spend.

### Refresh

Once the last batch is live, we make the next one from what the data shows.

## Who it fits {.why}

Brands already spending on streaming TV who want new creative every month without a production budget.

### Streaming spend

Already on CTV.

You run streaming TV through a platform or an agency and want more creative in rotation.

### Testing

More ads, more tests.

New angles every month, not one spot a year.

### Cash

Production stops coming first.

The cost of the creative moves with the media, not ahead of it.

## What you get {.why}

The same team and the same craft as our creative packages.

### The ads

Made to order.

Standard, Premium or Signature CTV ads built from your social content, exported for every platform you run on. [See the packages](/creative).

### The rhythm

A new batch, on schedule.

The next batch starts once the last one is live, so the rotation never goes stale.

### The view

One shared number.

Tagged spend shows us both what ran, and the results show what to make next.

## Common questions {.faq}

### How is the plan priced?

As a share of the media spend the ads run on, billed monthly. We quote the rate and the commitment on the intro call.

### Who owns the ads?

Screenbridge does, while the plan runs. If you want to own your ads outright, buy them as a creative project instead. [See the packages](/creative).

### What counts as spend?

The media spend tagged to our ads on your streaming buy. We set up the tagging with you before launch.

### Do I have to run my media through Reach?

No. The plan works with your own streaming buy. If you want us to run the media too, [Reach includes the ads](/reach).

### How fast is the first batch?

On the same timelines as our creative packages: 10, 15 or 20 business days, depending on the tier.

### Is there a minimum?

Yes. The plan fits brands that already spend steadily on streaming TV. We will tell you on the intro call whether it fits yours.

## See if the plan fits your spend. {.close}

Book a 15-minute call and we will look at your streaming buy together. Or write to [info@screenbridge.tv](mailto:info@screenbridge.tv).
