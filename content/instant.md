# Make TV ads from the videos you already have.

Screenbridge Instant is the self-serve tool for in-house teams. Upload a UGC video or social ad, and Instant turns it into TV-ready versions you can test on your own streaming buy.

## How it works {.loop}

One video in, three TV versions out.

### Upload

One UGC video or social ad, plus your logo.

### Pick

Three 30-second TV versions, each opening a different way.

### Export

Sized for CTV, YouTube and the platforms you run on.

### Test

Run them on your own buy and keep the one that wins.

## Who it is for {.why}

Brands and businesses that want to make their own TV ads, with our craft built in.

### In-house teams

Your team, our playbook.

Brands that run CTV themselves get a tool built on how Screenbridge makes ads, without handing off the work.

### Smaller budgets

Start with what you have.

No production budget needed. Your existing videos become TV ads you can test this month.

### Testing TV

Try it before you scale it.

A quick, low-cost way to see how your message works on the big screen before you commit to more.

## Common questions {.faq}

### Is Instant the same as a Screenbridge ad?

No. Instant is a self-serve tool that follows our templates and rules. A Screenbridge ad is made to order by our creative team, with custom design and animation. [See the packages](/creative).

### Do I have to run my media through Screenbridge?

No. Instant is for teams that run their own streaming buy. If you want us to run the media, [Reach includes the ads](/reach).

### What do I need to start?

A UGC video or social ad you have the rights to use, and your logo.

### Can I upgrade a version?

Yes. Send any Instant version to our team and we will build it into a Premium or Signature ad.

### When can I use it?

Instant is in early access. Book a call or write to us and we will tell you when it opens.
