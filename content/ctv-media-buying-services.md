Screenbridge+ is Screenbridge’s integrated creative + media management program. It combines premium, made-to-order CTV creative with full media buying, optimization, and performance strategy, all under one system. Screenbridge+ uses a simple, performance-aligned model: When you run your media through Screenbridge or one of our partners, your creative is included. #### What type of creative does Screenbridge produce?

Screenbridge produces custom, made-to-order CTV ads created by our in-house team of editors, animators, producers, and writers with thousands of real ad experience. No cheap AI Templates. No shortcuts.Every ad is crafted to be premium, on-brand, and designed for maximum performance.

#### What are Screenbridge’s creative tiers?

Screenbridge offers three tiers of creative, each designed for different goals: Standard: Ideal for turning a single piece of strong UGC into a clean, performance-focused CTV spot. Premium: A more elevated, storytelling-driven version of your UGC, using best practices for CTV structure, pacing, and brand polish. Signature: Our highest-end tier, designed for campaigns that need multiple personas, multiple products, narrative layers, and enhanced branding elements. Depending on your media spend tier, one of these is included in your Screenbridge+ program.

#### How fast does Screenbridge deliver a finished CTV ad?

Most brands receive their full creative package in a few days to a few weeks, depending on the complexity of the ad and the speed at which assets are provided.This is significantly faster than traditional video production, which often takes 6–12 weeks.

#### Does Screenbridge use AI to make the ads?

No, Screenbridge is not an AI video generator. Your ads are crafted by the same award-winning creative team behind Filmkraft.tv, using real editors, animators, producers, and copywriters. We do use selective AI tools behind the scenes to speed up workflows and generate creative elements when the quality is maintained (like AI Voiceovers).
