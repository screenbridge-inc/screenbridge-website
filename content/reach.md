A pixel on your site ties impressions to visits and conversions. You get a report built for your campaign, and the same numbers decide what the next cut tests.

The results we publish on [our work](/work) are creative results reported by clients. Reach reports are your own campaign's numbers.

## Common questions {.faq}

### What is Reach?

Reach is how Screenbridge runs CTV campaigns. It combines made-to-order CTV creative with media buying, optimization and reporting, all under one team, so the people who run your campaign are the people who make your next cut.

### How does pricing work?

When you run your media through Reach, your creative is included. You commit to a monthly media budget, the media is bought directly from supply, and the price is the exchange fee plus one Screenbridge fee. We recommend the right budget on the intro call.

### What creative is included?

A 30-second CTV commercial and a 15-second cut-down, with optional short-form variations, alternate hooks and alternate end cards, exported for every platform the campaign runs on. As results come in, we build new cuts from what the data shows.

### Where do the ads run?

On connected TV apps and streaming publishers, bought directly through the exchange. Specific networks, programs and premium placements are available through direct deals, chosen around your goals.

### How do I see what the campaign did?

A pixel on your site ties impressions to visits and conversions. You get a report built for your campaign, and the same numbers decide what the next cut tests.

### Is there a minimum spend?

Yes. Minimums vary by plan, from local businesses to brands spending six figures a month. We will recommend the right plan on the intro call.

### Does Screenbridge use AI to make the ads?

No, Screenbridge is not an AI video generator. Your ads are crafted by the same award-winning creative team behind Filmkraft.tv, using real editors, animators, producers, and copywriters. We do use selective AI tools behind the scenes to speed up workflows and generate creative elements when the quality is maintained (like AI Voiceovers).

### How do I get started?

Plan a campaign on [reach.screenbridge.tv](https://reach.screenbridge.tv), or book a quick call. Share a few pieces of UGC or your top-performing social ads, and our team will show you how Screenbridge can turn them into premium CTV creative and launch a campaign.

## Start with the campaign. {.close-reach}

Tell us your goals and budget and we will build the plan, with the ads included. Or write to [info@screenbridge.tv](mailto:info@screenbridge.tv).
