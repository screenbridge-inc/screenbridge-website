# Screenbridge is a New York video agency that makes UGC-style TV ads and runs them on CTV.

We turn the social content a brand already has into ads made for the TV screen, then buy the streaming media they run on.

## The facts {.facts}

The short version, for people and for search engines.

### What we make

UGC-style CTV ads in three packages: Standard, Premium and Signature. [See the packages](/creative).

### What we run

CTV campaigns through [Reach](/reach), with the ads included.

### Where we are

New York, working with brands across the US.

### Who makes the ads

Screenbridge is powered by [Filmkraft.tv](https://www.filmkraft.tv/), the creative video agency behind countless successful CTV campaigns.

### How to reach us

[info@screenbridge.tv](mailto:info@screenbridge.tv), or [book a call](/contact).
